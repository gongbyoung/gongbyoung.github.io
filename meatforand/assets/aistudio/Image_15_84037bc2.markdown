## Brand & Style

### Brand Personality & Philosophy
This design system powers enterprise logistics operations, field distribution hubs, and cross-docking workflows. The core aesthetic is engineered for operational speed, systematic reliability, and zero-ambiguity status recognition in high-friction mobile environments (e.g., warehouse floors, dock bays, delivery vans).

### Visual Movement
**Industrial Corporate Modern**: High-density utility balanced with modern crispness. It eschews decorative flourishes in favor of structured data tiers, precise contrast ratios, functional line separators, and unmistakable tactile tap targets. Micro-surfaces rely on subtle tonal distinctions rather than heavy shadows to maintain screen legibility under fluctuating warehouse lighting conditions.

## Colors

### Primary Palette
- **Deep Navy (`#1E3A8A`)**: Primary surface anchor, top application bars, high-level headers, and high-impact system CTAs. Communicates enterprise stability and operational authority.
- **Cobalt Blue (`#2563EB`)**: Interactive primary color for active states, key workflows (e.g., "Scan Batch", "Confirm Dispatch"), and link indicators.

### Integration Accent
- **Kakao Yellow (`#FEE500`)**: Reserved exclusively for KakaoTalk notifications, quick share intents, and direct messenger integrations. Paired strictly with Dark Cocoa text (`#3C1E1E`) to satisfy strict accessibility and contrast standards.

### Operational Semantic Palette
- **Inbound / Success Green (`#16A34A` / Light BG: `#DCFCE7`)**: Finished sorting, successful scan, completed receipt, inbound normal.
- **Transit / Warning Orange (`#EA580C` / Light BG: `#FFEDD5`)**: Pending verification, cross-dock transit, stock warning, low inventory.
- **Outbound Issue / Critical Red (`#DC2626` / Light BG: `#FEE2E2`)**: Damaged shipment, SKU mismatch, critical stockout, delayed route.

### Neutral & Tonal Palette
- **Base Canvas (`#F8FAFC`)**: Light slate background providing contrast for elevated white surfaces.
- **Card & Container Surface (`#FFFFFF`)**: Pure white base for inventory cards and metrics modules.
- **Border Tone (`#E2E8F0`)**: Low-contrast structural borders for field separators and table cells.
- **Text Tones**: Headings (`#0F172A`), Body Text (`#334155`), Muted/Metadata (`#64748B`).

## Typography

### Structural Decisions
- **Headings & Body (`Hanken Grotesk` / Korean system fallback `Pretendard`)**: Balanced geometric grotesque optimized for high-speed scanning and multi-lingual Latin/Hangul log numbers.
- **Technical & Monospace Fields (`JetBrains Mono`)**: Applied to all SKU codes, batch numbers, barcode digits, timestamps, and real-time inventory count integers to eliminate glyph confusion (e.g., differentiating `0` and `O`, `1` and `l`).

### Mobile Adaptation Rules
All typography levels maintain minimum 1.25x line height to prevent touch collision and optical bleeding on handheld barcode terminals or mobile screens with screen protectors.

## Layout & Spacing

### Mobile-First Fluid Grid
- **Screen Margins**: Strict `16px` (`1rem`) lateral margin for all viewport edges to maximize usable density on 360px–428px device widths.
- **Component Gutters**: `12px` (`0.75rem`) horizontal separation between multi-column stat tiles and twin metric indicators.
- **Vertical Rhythm**: Built upon a `4px` baseline unit. Component internals scale linearly (`4px`, `8px`, `12px`, `16px`, `24px`).

### Adaptation Breakpoints
- **Mobile Handheld (`< 600px`)**: Single-column vertical stream for inventory listings and full-width barcode scanner viewports.
- **Tablet / Mounted Terminal (`600px - 1024px`)**: Splits into a 2-column workspace featuring an order list pane (40%) and an item verification/pallet layout pane (60%).

## Elevation & Depth

### Tonal Stratification
Visual depth is established primarily through crisp boundary borders and slight tonal shift, rather than heavy drop shadows which reduce readability under high glare.

- **Level 0 (Canvas Base)**: `#F8FAFC` — Primary application background.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Surface)**: `#FFFFFF` — Inset with a `1px` border of `#E2E8F0`. Shadow: `0 1px 2px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Active/Selected Card, Pull-down Drawer)**: `#FFFFFF` — Border: `#CBD5E1`. Shadow: `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Barcode Scanner Overlay, Modals, Action Sheets)**: Elevated modal sheet with `#0F172A` backdrop tint at `40%` opacity. Shadow: `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

### Shape Language
This design system utilizes **Soft (`1`)** roundedness. Precision logistics demands maximum screen real estate utilization and structured modularity; overly rounded pill shapes waste critical pixel boundaries in compact mobile tables and data-dense cards.

- **Micro Components (Badges, SKU Tags, Buttons)**: `4px` (`rounded-sm`).
- **Containers & Cards (Shipment Tiles, Table Wrappers)**: `8px` (`rounded-lg`).
- **Bottom Sheets & Modal Surfaces**: `12px` (`rounded-xl`) applied to the top edge corners only.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Operational Button**: Background `#2563EB`, text `#FFFFFF`, height `48px` (touch-target compliant), corner radius `4px`, font weight `600`. Active state darkens to `#1E3A8A`.
- **Secondary / Action Button**: Background `#EFF6FF`, border `1px solid #BFDBFE`, text `#1E3A8A`.
- **Kakao Integration Action**: Background `#FEE500`, text `#3C1E1E`, font weight `600`. Employs the Kakao chat balloon icon on the left with `8px` padding.

### Chips & Semantic Status Badges
- Compact badges designed for instant sorting identification:
  - **Inbound Finished**: Text `#16A34A`, background `#DCFCE7`, border `1px solid #BBF7D0`.
  - **In Transit / Review**: Text `#C2410C`, background `#FFEDD5`, border `1px solid #FED7AA`.
  - **Discrepancy / Alert**: Text `#DC2626`, background `#FEE2E2`, border `1px solid #FECACA`.
  - SKU & Rack location codes are rendered in `JetBrains Mono` with uppercase tags and `#F1F5F9` neutral backing.

### Cards (Shipment & SKU Card)
- Surface `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, inner padding `12px`.
- Composed of:
  1. Top metadata line (Timestamp + Location code).
  2. Main line (SKU / Product Name in bold with quantity indicator in monospace).
  3. Bottom operational tray containing status chip and quick actions.

### Data Tables & Compact Lists
- Alternating row zebra striping using `#FFFFFF` and `#F8FAFC`.
- Sticky column headers with `#F1F5F9` background, `11px` uppercase typography, and `1px` bottom border `#CBD5E1`.
- Row height fixed at `40px` for high-density listing, with full-row tap targets triggering detail side-sheets.

### Inputs & Barcode Trigger Fields
- Inputs feature `44px` height, `#FFFFFF` background, and `1px solid #CBD5E1` border.
- Active focus state applies a `2px` focus ring in `#2563EB` with zero horizontal layout shift.
- Trailing accessory slot holds a high-contrast `#1E3A8A` barcode icon button for instant scanner launch.

### Progress & Stock Level Charts
- High-contrast, flat linear bars with neutral tracks (`#E2E8F0`) and solid semantic fills without gradients.
- Micro sparklines feature `2px` stroke width and distinct circular nodes for peak delivery load tracking.
